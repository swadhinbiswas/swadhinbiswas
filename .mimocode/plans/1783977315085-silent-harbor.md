# Black & White Redesign with Colored Icons

## Goal
Convert the entire site to a monochrome (black & white) palette while keeping skill/tech icons in full color. Add light mode support via `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Phase 3: Icon Preservation

**No changes needed for icons.** Skill icons are `<img>` tags loading SVGs from CDN — they render in full color regardless of CSS. The `.skill-icon` and `.skill-tile-img` classes have no `filter: grayscale()` applied, so icons will naturally stay colored against the monochrome UI.

Verify this by checking:
- `src/pages/skills.astro` line 155: `<img src={iconUrl} ... class="skill-icon" />`
- `src/pages/skills.astro` line 266: `.skill-icon { filter: brightness(1.1); }` — no grayscale, safe
